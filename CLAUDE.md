## Project notes

- Data lives in the Supabase database shared with the AI SDR app (`DATABASE_URL`, transaction pooler).
  - This app owns only `dash_*` tables and views. Migrations in `db/migrations` must stay additive.
  - Read AI SDR tables (`companies`, `ctx_events`, `sdr_users`) but never alter them.
  - Never run session-level `SET` through the pooler; it leaks into other clients. `lib/db.js` runs writes in `BEGIN READ WRITE` transactions.
- `lib/sync`, `lib/metrics`, `lib/outreach` and `lib/db.js` are shared by route handlers and plain-node scripts, so they use relative imports with `.js` extensions (no `@/`).
- Commands:
  - `npm run db:migrate`
  - `npm run sync` (add `-- --full` to backfill, or name a job)
  - `npm run import:meetings`
- Attribution: SDR, program and theme are parsed from campaign names (`lib/outreach/campaign-parser.js`) and corrected with `dash_campaign_overrides`. HeyReach sender accounts are senior people, not SDRs.
- Two Instantly workspaces: the original (`INSTANTLY_API_KEY`) and the AI SDR send workspace (`INSTANTLY_SEND_API_KEY`), listed in `lib/outreach/instantly.js` as `WORKSPACES`. A workspace answers only to its own key, so every campaign, analytics, leads and steps job loops `configuredWorkspaces()`, and `dash_campaigns.workspace` says which key a campaign needs. In the archive they are `ctx_events.source` 'instantly' and 'instantly_aisdr' — use `INSTANTLY_SOURCES` from `lib/outreach/email-sources.js`, never the bare string, or the second workspace's replies go unlabelled and uncounted. Reading only the first one hid 43 campaigns, 811 sent emails and 14 opportunities while every job reported itself complete.
- Who pulls what: the AI SDR app pulls message content (Instantly emails, HeyReach conversations) into `ctx_events` from its own daily cron. This app never calls Instantly `/emails` and never copies reply text; `reply-verdicts` and `reply-classify` read the archive and store only labels.
- Reply labels: GPT-4.1 mini (`lib/reply-llm.js`) labels a reply once per md5 fingerprint of its archived text and `PROMPT_VERSION`. Metrics and the AI SDR app read labels through `dash_v_reply_verdicts`.
- HeyReach is read-only and throttled to ~100 requests/min in `lib/outreach/heyreach.js`; keep every HeyReach call a read. `heyreach-leads` stores each campaign's people in `dash_linkedin_leads`, re-reading a campaign only while it runs or when its status changes.
- Companies across channels: a LinkedIn person's company is resolved to a domain by `lib/outreach/linkedin.js`; admin mappings in `dash_company_aliases` override it through `dash_v_linkedin_leads`. The company is the unit email and LinkedIn share.
- Meetings have two sources: `meetings-slack` parses the booking bot's Slack alerts out of `ctx_events` (no API call, one windowed query) into `dash_meetings` rows tagged `source = 'slack'`, and `meetings-csv` replaces only the `source = 'sheet'` rows. `dash_v_meetings` merges them, keeping the sheet's row when both describe a company within three days, and attributes the campaign from email or LinkedIn outreach. Anything that counts meetings reads the view, never `dash_meetings`.
- The booking alerts live in Slack `#sales-sdr-outreach` (C05TANDC64F). That channel has never been on the AI SDR app's nightly cron: its `SLACK_CHANNELS` in Vercel lists the ICP-decisions channel instead, and `ctx_sources.config.channels` is empty, so the env var alone decides. Every booking row before 12 Oct 2026 was written by someone running that sync from a laptop, where `.env.local` does list it. Until that env var includes `C05TANDC64F`, the gap reopens the day after each manual backfill. Its saved cursor is also frozen at March 2024, so a manual "Sync" press resumes in 2024 and looks like an empty channel — clear it with `UPDATE ctx_sources SET cursor = NULL WHERE key = 'slack'`.
- Meetings booked come from a waterfall of three sources, all writing `dash_meetings` with `source` saying which: the audit sheet (`meetings-csv`, the only source of qualified and deal value), the booking bot's Slack alert (`meetings-slack`), and HubSpot (`meetings-hubspot`, a first meeting at a company we had already contacted, only where neither of the others has it). The Slack alert channel went unarchived upstream from 2026-09-22, which is why the HubSpot rung exists.
- The booking alert also carries a pre-meeting screen the bot started posting in October 2026 (`booking_qualified`, `qualification_note`, `booking_action`, `booked_via`, `heard_from` on `dash_meetings`, parsed by `parseQualification`). It is captured but not shown anywhere yet, and it is NOT `dash_meetings.qualified`, which is the audit sheet's post-meeting verdict behind qualified pipeline. The screen currently reaches only inbound and marketing bookings from the web demo form.
- Whether a meeting was held is decided by `meetings-evidence` (`lib/sync/meeting-evidence.js`), not by the booking: the audit sheet's verdict, else a Fireflies recording within a day with someone whose email is at that company in the room, else the sales pipeline moving the company on afterwards. Nothing proven means `review` ("No show?"), never held — HubSpot's COMPLETED is not evidence, because an internal POC waiting alone on the call sets it. A booking that moved keeps its row with `superseded_by` set, counts as `rescheduled`, and is left out of every total (`NOT m.rescheduled`). Held rate is held over the meetings that are not still upcoming.
- An SDR's own word on a meeting lives in columns no sync rewrites: `campaign_id` (016), and `stated_sdr` (020, added 2026-10-28 — `sdr_name` is inside MEETING_COLUMNS, so meetings-slack/csv/hubspot overwrite it within their windows). `/api/meetings/manual` (POST add, PATCH attribute any booking, DELETE manual rows) is open to any signed-in user and writes there; `dash_v_meetings` reads `stated_sdr` first and treats it as booking_by = 'sdr'.
- HubSpot already holds an SDR meeting form, on DEALS, not meetings: `date_of_meeting_sdr_form` (date) and `sdr_owner` (Nitin | Akhil | Muni | Ads | Inbound | Referral | Outbound - Old | Sanika | Aaryan | Akash), 463 deals filled as of 2026-10-28. The meetings object has zero custom properties and the connector pulls only 5 of them; the forms API 403s on scope; this app has no HUBSPOT_ACCESS_TOKEN. So consuming it needs either that token here or `deals` added to the AI SDR connector's OBJECT_TYPES. Nitin and Aaryan are not on dash_team.
- Meeting credit has one definition, in `dash_v_meetings`: `booking_by` (sdr | other | unknown) and `attribution` (sourced | influenced | none). `campaign_id`, `campaign_name`, `campaign_sdr`, `program`, `theme` and `attributed_sdr` are populated for sourced meetings only. Ayush, 2026-10-27: an inbound booking at an emailed company was showing as that SDR's meeting.
- The dashboard shows ONLY meetings SDR outreach booked (Ayush, 2026-11-05: "only SDR outreaches are important here"): no AE, inbound, referral or marketing meeting is counted, listed or flagged, and the "came in another way" (influenced) flags are gone. `OUTREACH_MEETING` in `lib/metrics/sql.js` is the rule; `scope('meeting')` adds it to every scoped query, and the queries that join meetings by company domain (companies, coverage, campaigns, linkedin, programs) call it. tests/outreach-meetings.test.mjs fails on a second rule. It replaced listMeetings' own "outbound direction or a campaign match", which let AE meetings into the Overview (October: 31 shown, 14 SDR-booked). The one exception is `unclaimedMeetings` (booking_by 'unknown'), offered on the Meetings page only so an SDR can claim one; it counts nowhere until claimed.
- SDR positives ARE the positive response sheet (Ayush, 2026-11-05: "ground truth for sdrs"). AI SDR and every other campaign stay on our reply labels, kept separate: a total is the sheet's count plus the labels' count, with no dedupe between them.
  - `positive-sheet` (`lib/sync/jobs/positive-sheet.js`, parsing and matching pure in `lib/outreach/positive-sheet.js`) reads two tabs daily with the `icp-dashboard@` service account, read-only, and rewrites `dash_sdr_positives` (026): the old tracker `Responses - 2024` and `Positive responses` in the assignment sheet. It refuses to apply a read under 80% of what is stored, so an emptied or renamed tab cannot wipe every SDR's positives. Needs `GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_B64` on Vercel; without it `lib/metrics/meta.js` warns on every page.
  - Whose campaigns: `dash_team.logs_positives`, read as `dash_v_campaigns.positives_from_sheet`: the six SDRs plus Sanika and Akash, who logged there. Muni, Ayush and Muskan are not, so their campaigns keep the labels. A new SDR needs this set by hand; the admin screen does not offer it.
  - In those campaigns a lead's `positive` is "a sheet row points at this lead", in both `dash_v_leads` and `dash_v_linkedin_leads`, and on the sheet also means replied and not negative, whatever the archive holds. Instantly interest marks and reply labels are not read there. Period counts come from `sheetOutcomes` in sql.js (one row per sheet row, the SDR the sheet credits), which `emailOutcomeEvents` and `linkedinOutcomeEvents` union in, so a row counts for its SDR and the totals even with no lead. `campaign_id` and `lead_id` are set only when the person was reached by an SDR-owned, non-excluded campaign by the day of the reply; without them the row counts nowhere on campaign or program pages. Company tables add such rows through `sheet_only` in `companyCtes` and `SHEET_POSITIVE_AT_COMPANY`.
  - Traps in the tabs: dates have no year (placed from row order, `placeYear`; 170 of 193 people land within a day of the archive); the old tab's "Week starting" says 2025 for 2026 rows and is not read; a row moved between tabs is one positive, keeping its first date, but someone logged twice months apart is two (40 people); "Nov-26" on a row followed up on Aug-20 is a typo, while a reply two months after its week is real.
  - Rows are skipped only where the SDR said so: the old tab's Neutral / Negative mark, or a note saying "not positive", "neutral reply" or "not counted" (25 rows); plus 4 marked inbound or referral. "Unlikely to book" is what happened later, not what the reply was, so those count.
  - Showing what they said: `positiveResponses` (lib/metrics/positives.js) matches each row to that person's reply within 30 days, from `dash_v_reply_verdicts`, else shows the SDR's note. Only someone the name could belong to is matched: a colleague replying that week must not have their words shown under the logged name (`initialFits`). The per-reply labels (`VERDICTS`, Latest replies, the conversation panel) are still our model's reading of that reply, so a reply can read Interested with no positive counted.
- Views live in `900_dash_views.sql`, which is re-applied on every migrate and sorts after every table migration.
- The Overview (`lib/metrics/dashboard.js`, served by `/api/metrics/overview`) counts the same way a program page does: unique companies, a channel toggle (both | email | linkedin), and meetings narrowed by the attributed campaign's platform. `getOverview` stays for the SDR detail page, which spreads it.
- `dash_v_meetings` costs about six seconds a query whatever the range, because its attribution lateral re-runs `dash_v_linkedin_leads` per meeting. A page that needs several cuts of it reads the rows once and counts them with `totalsFromMeetings` / `groupMeetings`.
- Company counts come from `companyPanel` (one query: the funnel summary aggregated in SQL plus the companies that replied or met), so a wide range costs an aggregate instead of thousands of rows. `companiesAcrossChannels` still returns every row for program pages.
- Program pages (`getProgramDetail`) default to everything since the program's first campaign and take `channel` (both | email | linkedin). Conversations (`lib/metrics/conversation.js`) read message text from `ctx_events` when someone opens one; never copy that text into `dash_*` tables.
- Companies reached in a range are counted at their FIRST touch, never a follow-up (Ayush, 2026-11-02): `dash_v_leads.first_contact_at` (the earliest of the archive's first send, filled by `email-first-touch`, and every last-contact time a leads sync has seen) and `dash_v_linkedin_leads.first_touch_at` (the latest action while it is still the only one, else `added_at`). Ranges used to read the last contact, which ran weekly company counts 18-36% low. `companyCtes` in `lib/metrics/companies.js` is the only place that bounds a range for companies; tests/first-touch.test.mjs fails if a metrics query bounds on `last_contact_at` again.
- A LinkedIn person counts toward a company only once their employer has a domain (`LI_IS_COMPANY`). People at name-only employers are still people reached, and travel beside the funnel as `summary.unresolved` ({ people, employers }); an admin alias in Data health makes one a company.
- Instantly jobs delete and walk only the workspaces this deployment holds a key for (`configuredWorkspaceIds()`). Without that scope, `instantly-periods` wiped the AI SDR workspace's weeks on every run while prod lacked `INSTANTLY_SEND_API_KEY`.
- Merges the company-identity job cannot prove are decided by hand: shared-root groups by setting `dash_domain_merge_review.status`, and one company under different roots by a `source = 'admin'` row in `dash_company_domains` (023 and 024 hold the reviewed sets). The job also honours merges made in the ICP dashboard (`companies.merged_into_id`, `domain_aliases` rows with `source = 'manual_merge'`) as `source = 'icp'`; its other alias rows are import-time guesses and are not read. As of 2026-11-04 the ICP dashboard had merged nothing (27.5k candidates pending), so ours are the only decisions. No rule tested safe to automate: about half of shared-root groups are different companies, and acquired companies inherit the buyer's registry name. Acquisitions stay separate.
- Whether a meeting was held is not shown anywhere in the UI (Ayush, 2026-11-03): no held tile, column, chart series, funnel step or "No show?" count. `meetings-evidence` still computes it and the metrics still return it; the meetings table shows only Upcoming / Rescheduled. The Overview has no company funnel card either (same date); program pages still do.
- The dashboard is an OPEN view (Ayush, 2026-11-04), never a cohort: a range holds everything that happened in it, whenever the person was reached, and each thing is counted once, on its own day.
  - Replies at the person's first reply, positive and said-no at the first reply labelled so (or, in SDR campaigns, positive on the day the sheet logs): `first_reply_at`, `positive_at`, `negative_at` in both lead views, falling back to Instantly's last reply / HeyReach's last action until the archive has the reply. `emailOutcomeEvents` (core.js) and `linkedinOutcomeEvents` (linkedin.js) give each outcome its own row dated `OUTCOME_AT`; `leadOutcomes`, `linkedinOutcomes`, campaign period results and the step breakdown all read them.
  - Volumes are the first touch only: `sent` / `emailsSent` is Instantly's new leads contacted (first emails), `allSent` has the follow-ups too; LinkedIn messages are the first message to each person (`dash_linkedin_leads.first_message_at`, 025, filled from the archive by `linkedin-first-message`, which also refills what `heyreach-leads` clears when it rewrites a campaign). HeyReach's `messages_sent` counter is never read.
  - Meetings by meeting date. Companies reached in a range: any company where someone got their first email or invite in it.
  - Every number carries its definition, worded once in `components/views/definitions.js` (tile notes, card subtitles, `hint` on DataTable columns).
  - The channel cards (Overview and program pages, `emailCard` / `linkedinCard` in programs.js) show four numbers each, as Ayush chose on 2026-11-05. Email: Emails sent (`allSent`, follow-ups included, and the card says so), People emailed (`peopleEmailed`: unique people whose first email fell in the range), Positive responses, Meetings booked (SDR-booked, credited to an Instantly campaign). LinkedIn: invites sent, invites accepted, Positive responses, Meetings booked (credited to a HeyReach campaign). SDR and program tables still say "First emails".
