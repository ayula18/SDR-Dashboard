## Project notes

- Data lives in the Supabase database shared with the AI SDR app (`DATABASE_URL`, transaction pooler).
  - This app owns only `dash_*` tables and views. Migrations in `db/migrations` must stay additive.
  - Read AI SDR tables (`companies`, `ctx_events`, `sdr_users`) but never alter them.
  - Never run session-level `SET` through the pooler; it leaks into other clients. `lib/db.js` runs writes in `BEGIN READ WRITE` transactions.
- `lib/sync`, `lib/metrics`, `lib/outreach` and `lib/db.js` are shared by route handlers and plain-node scripts, so they use relative imports with `.js` extensions (no `@/`).
- Commands:
  - `npm run db:migrate`
  - `npm run sync` (add `-- --full` to backfill, or name a job)
  - `npm run import:meetings`
- Attribution: SDR, program and theme are parsed from campaign names (`lib/outreach/campaign-parser.js`) and corrected with `dash_campaign_overrides`. HeyReach sender accounts are senior people, not SDRs.
- Two Instantly workspaces: the original (`INSTANTLY_API_KEY`) and the AI SDR send workspace (`INSTANTLY_SEND_API_KEY`), listed in `lib/outreach/instantly.js` as `WORKSPACES`. A workspace answers only to its own key, so every campaign, analytics, leads and steps job loops `configuredWorkspaces()`, and `dash_campaigns.workspace` says which key a campaign needs. In the archive they are `ctx_events.source` 'instantly' and 'instantly_aisdr' — use `INSTANTLY_SOURCES` from `lib/outreach/email-sources.js`, never the bare string, or the second workspace's replies go unlabelled and uncounted. Reading only the first one hid 43 campaigns, 811 sent emails and 14 opportunities while every job reported itself complete.
- Who pulls what: the AI SDR app pulls message content (Instantly emails, HeyReach conversations) into `ctx_events` from its own daily cron. This app never calls Instantly `/emails` and never copies reply text; `reply-verdicts` and `reply-classify` read the archive and store only labels.
- Reply labels: GPT-4.1 mini (`lib/reply-llm.js`) labels a reply once per md5 fingerprint of its archived text and `PROMPT_VERSION`. Metrics and the AI SDR app read labels through `dash_v_reply_verdicts`.
- HeyReach is read-only and throttled to ~100 requests/min in `lib/outreach/heyreach.js`; keep every HeyReach call a read. `heyreach-leads` stores each campaign's people in `dash_linkedin_leads`, re-reading a campaign only while it runs or when its status changes.
- Companies across channels: a LinkedIn person's company is resolved to a domain by `lib/outreach/linkedin.js`; admin mappings in `dash_company_aliases` override it through `dash_v_linkedin_leads`. The company is the unit email and LinkedIn share.
- Meetings have two sources: `meetings-slack` parses the booking bot's Slack alerts out of `ctx_events` (no API call, one windowed query) into `dash_meetings` rows tagged `source = 'slack'`, and `meetings-csv` replaces only the `source = 'sheet'` rows. `dash_v_meetings` merges them, keeping the sheet's row when both describe a company within three days, and attributes the campaign from email or LinkedIn outreach. Anything that counts meetings reads the view, never `dash_meetings`.
- The booking alerts live in Slack `#sales-sdr-outreach` (C05TANDC64F). That channel has never been on the AI SDR app's nightly cron: its `SLACK_CHANNELS` in Vercel lists the ICP-decisions channel instead, and `ctx_sources.config.channels` is empty, so the env var alone decides. Every booking row before 12 Oct 2026 was written by someone running that sync from a laptop, where `.env.local` does list it. Until that env var includes `C05TANDC64F`, the gap reopens the day after each manual backfill. Its saved cursor is also frozen at March 2024, so a manual "Sync" press resumes in 2024 and looks like an empty channel — clear it with `UPDATE ctx_sources SET cursor = NULL WHERE key = 'slack'`.
- Meetings booked come from a waterfall of three sources, all writing `dash_meetings` with `source` saying which: the audit sheet (`meetings-csv`, the only source of qualified and deal value), the booking bot's Slack alert (`meetings-slack`), and HubSpot (`meetings-hubspot`, a first meeting at a company we had already contacted, only where neither of the others has it). The Slack alert channel went unarchived upstream from 2026-09-22, which is why the HubSpot rung exists.
- The booking alert also carries a pre-meeting screen the bot started posting in October 2026 (`booking_qualified`, `qualification_note`, `booking_action`, `booked_via`, `heard_from` on `dash_meetings`, parsed by `parseQualification`). It is captured but not shown anywhere yet, and it is NOT `dash_meetings.qualified`, which is the audit sheet's post-meeting verdict behind qualified pipeline. The screen currently reaches only inbound and marketing bookings from the web demo form.
- Whether a meeting was held is decided by `meetings-evidence` (`lib/sync/meeting-evidence.js`), not by the booking: the audit sheet's verdict, else a Fireflies recording within a day with someone whose email is at that company in the room, else the sales pipeline moving the company on afterwards. Nothing proven means `review` ("No show?"), never held — HubSpot's COMPLETED is not evidence, because an internal POC waiting alone on the call sets it. A booking that moved keeps its row with `superseded_by` set, counts as `rescheduled`, and is left out of every total (`NOT m.rescheduled`). Held rate is held over the meetings that are not still upcoming.
- An SDR's own word on a meeting lives in columns no sync rewrites: `campaign_id` (016), and `stated_sdr` (020, added 2026-10-28 — `sdr_name` is inside MEETING_COLUMNS, so meetings-slack/csv/hubspot overwrite it within their windows). `/api/meetings/manual` (POST add, PATCH attribute any booking, DELETE manual rows) is open to any signed-in user and writes there; `dash_v_meetings` reads `stated_sdr` first and treats it as booking_by = 'sdr'.
- HubSpot already holds an SDR meeting form, on DEALS, not meetings: `date_of_meeting_sdr_form` (date) and `sdr_owner` (Nitin | Akhil | Muni | Ads | Inbound | Referral | Outbound - Old | Sanika | Aaryan | Akash), 463 deals filled as of 2026-10-28. The meetings object has zero custom properties and the connector pulls only 5 of them; the forms API 403s on scope; this app has no HUBSPOT_ACCESS_TOKEN. So consuming it needs either that token here or `deals` added to the AI SDR connector's OBJECT_TYPES. Nitin and Aaryan are not on dash_team.
- Meeting credit has one definition, in `dash_v_meetings`: `booking_by` (sdr | other | unknown) and `attribution` (sourced | influenced | none). `campaign_id`, `campaign_name`, `campaign_sdr`, `program`, `theme` and `attributed_sdr` are populated for sourced meetings only, so a query that filters or groups by them can never count a meeting outreach did not book; `influenced_*` carries the flagged link (within 30 days of the last touch) for display. Anything counting meetings by company domain must filter `attribution = 'sourced'` itself — companies.js, coverage.js, campaigns.js and linkedin.js do. Ayush, 2026-10-27: an inbound booking at an emailed company was showing as that SDR's meeting.
- Views live in `900_dash_views.sql`, which is re-applied on every migrate and sorts after every table migration.
- The Overview (`lib/metrics/dashboard.js`, served by `/api/metrics/overview`) counts the same way a program page does: unique companies, a channel toggle (both | email | linkedin), and meetings narrowed by the attributed campaign's platform. `getOverview` stays for the SDR detail page, which spreads it.
- `dash_v_meetings` costs about six seconds a query whatever the range, because its attribution lateral re-runs `dash_v_linkedin_leads` per meeting. A page that needs several cuts of it reads the rows once and counts them with `totalsFromMeetings` / `groupMeetings`.
- Company counts come from `companyPanel` (one query: the funnel summary aggregated in SQL plus the companies that replied or met), so a wide range costs an aggregate instead of thousands of rows. `companiesAcrossChannels` still returns every row for program pages.
- Program pages (`getProgramDetail`) default to everything since the program's first campaign and take `channel` (both | email | linkedin). Conversations (`lib/metrics/conversation.js`) read message text from `ctx_events` when someone opens one; never copy that text into `dash_*` tables.
